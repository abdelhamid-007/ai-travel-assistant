Q1: How do I book a trip?
A1: You can book on our website, by phone or WhatsApp, or at our office. A booking is confirmed once the deposit is paid.

Q2: How much deposit do I need to pay?
A2: You pay a 30% deposit to confirm your booking. The rest must be paid at least 15 days before departure.

Q3: Which payment methods do you accept?
A3: We accept cash in Tunisian dinars, bank cards, and bank transfer. Umrah packages can be paid in up to 3 monthly installments.

Q4: What is your cancellation policy?
A4: Cancellation is free up to 30 days before departure. Between 30 and 15 days before departure, 50% of the price is charged. Less than 15 days before departure, there is no refund. For Umrah packages, the visa fee is never refunded once the visa is issued.

Q5: Do children get a discount?
A5: Children under 2 travel free without their own seat or bed. Children from 2 to 11 years old get 25% off the adult price, unless the offer gives a different child price.

Q6: Do I need a visa?
A6: Tunisian passport holders do not need a visa for Turkey or Morocco. For Egypt and the United Arab Emirates, we apply for the visa for you; send your documents at least 3 weeks before departure. The Umrah visa is included in all Umrah packages.

Q7: How long must my passport be valid?
A7: Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months after your return date. Trips inside Tunisia only need a national ID card.

Q8: Is there a supplement for a single room?
A8: Yes. A single room costs 25% more than the price per person in a double room.

Q9: Is travel insurance included?
A9: Travel insurance with medical cover is included in all international packages. It is not included in trips inside Tunisia, but we can add it for 25 DT per person.

Q10: Do you offer group discounts?
A10: Groups of 10 people or more get an 8% discount on most packages. Contact us for a group quote.

Q11: From which airport do flights leave?
A11: International flights leave from Tunis-Carthage airport. Departures from Djerba or Monastir are possible on request with a supplement.

Q12: How can I contact you?
A12: Call us or message us on WhatsApp from Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00. You can also use the contact form on our website.
